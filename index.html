Our Story

A Legacy Brewed in
Saffron & Spice

Born in the heart of Old Delhi in 1998, Royal Biryani House began as a humble family kitchen where Nani Ji's secret spice blends were guarded like crown jewels. Today, we carry those traditions forward — one dum-cooked pot at a time.

Each grain of basmati is aged for 18 months. Each masala is hand-ground at dawn. Every biryani that leaves our kitchen carries the warmth of a royal Mughal kitchen and the soul of home cooking.
