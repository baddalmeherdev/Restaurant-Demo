Our Story

A Legacy Brewed in
Saffron & Spice

Born in the walled lanes of Old Delhi in 1998, Royal Biryani House began as a single family hearth where Nani Ji's spice blends were guarded like crown jewels. Today those recipes feed thousands daily — unchanged, un-compromised, unapologetically royal.

Every grain of basmati is aged for 18 months. Every masala is hand-ground at dawn. Every pot of dum biryani is sealed with dough and kissed by live charcoal, exactly as it was in the Mughal kitchens of Emperor Akbar's era.
